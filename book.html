Requirements

Rental requirements.

These conditions apply to every rental and are set out in the rental agreement.

🪪 Driver

A valid standard driver's license is sufficient; no CDL is required. Additional drivers must be named on the rental agreement.

🛡️ Insurance

Proof of insurance required before pickup.

🚭 No smoking

Smoking and vaping are prohibited inside the vehicle at all times. A violation forfeits the security deposit.

🐾 Pets

Pet friendly — dogs welcome with a $125 fee. No cats.

🔇 Generator

6 generator hours / day included. Campground quiet hours, typically 10pm–7am, must be observed.

↩️ Return

6 PM end time. A 90-minute grace period applies, after which $50 per hour or part hour is charged.

Cancellations: Moderate policy — see the legal page for the current terms — except documented weather or mechanical issues, which we handle case-by-case and always have. Full text on the legal page.
