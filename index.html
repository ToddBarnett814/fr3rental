The RV

The Bunkhouse Floorplan.

A king bed, a full bed over the cab, two pull-out couches, a dinette conversion and bunks. Ten berths in total, which works out to roughly six adults in comfort. Complete specifications are on the next page.

The Dining Table faces the Couch rather than a wall — rare in a Class A, and the reason Dinner, Games and Hangout actually work in here. Swivel the cab seats around, add a few lawn chairs, and you can seat 12.
